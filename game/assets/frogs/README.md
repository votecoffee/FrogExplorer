Six self-contained animated frog GLBs are included, with their individual
source terms documented in ASSET_CREDITS.md and config/runtime_assets.lock.json.
The authored red_eye_original.glb includes original geometry, painted textures,
rig and 28 motions under MIT; rebuild it with scripts/frogs/build_original_red_eye.py.
The historical restricted red_eye.glb remains local and is not selected by default.
Texture authoring files and
original downloads stay local. Existing realistic models are not modified by
the fallback or the world tiling pipeline.
